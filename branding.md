5) Branding & Design Guidelines
Brand Personality: Warm, Hopeful, Youthful, Purpose-driven.
Tone: Positive, empowering, relatable (never corporate or intimidating).
Primary Color (Youth Blue): #0B6F9A (for headers, buttons, highlights).
Accent Colors:
Warm Coral: #FF7A59 (for icons, calls to action, progress rings).
Soft Yellow: #FFD66B (for section highlights, celebrations).
Typography:
Headings: Poppins Bold (modern and approachable).
Body Text: Lato Regular (clear and friendly).
Imagery: Authentic photos of diverse Kenyan teens in school settings. Simple, clean line icons
